https://www.experimental-history.com/p/i-like-em-thick I really like this article, and this idea of "good" art having depth. I think there is value in differentiating "good" from "not good" or "bad" art, and this seems like a useful metric. Looking back at art I have enjoyed, I think it is often "thick" in this way, something I can become engrossed in. I also agree that this is likely where bad art starts, something that can be immediately good, but then becomes empty on further examination.
