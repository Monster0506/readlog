https://jeremymorrell.dev/blog/extensible-software-in-the-age-of-llms/ I struggle to believe that "personal" or one-off software or dashboards will be a concept that businesses will adopt. The maintainability, long-term-value, security concerns, etc., etc. will far outweigh the short term benefit of being able to create a quick dashboard. For personal work, I absolutely believe that custom software is the way to go, but enterprise scale work is unlikely to be this way.
