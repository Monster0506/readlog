> Nobody is a genius, but sometimes people create works of genius, and that’s a much more useful way to think about things. It’s also a lot less pressure! You don’t have to worry about whether you qualify as a genius, or even whether you’re particularly smart: you just have to focus on creating the best stuff you can. Maybe some of that stuff will be good, and probably a lot of it will be bad, and maybe if you work really hard or things come together in a very specific way, you’ll create something that someone else might call a genius.
