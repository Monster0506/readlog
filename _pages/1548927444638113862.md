https://punyamishra.com/2026/04/16/why-sal-khant-on-learning-by-making-but-teaching-by-telling/ I am not a fan of this article as a whole. It doesn't really have a point other than "Khan Academy sucks", which I disagree with. Some people do learn from lecture effectively.  I learn MOST by doing, by immersion, by asking questions, etc. I don't learn well from other just telling me their earned knowledge without me going through those same steps. I will suggest, au contraire to the article, that Khan does a decent job of taking you through the intuition, such that there is some level of deeper engagement with the material.  This is the great difficulty of education, to impart previously acquired knowledge in a way that lets all students learn. This is also why tutoring is really effective.
